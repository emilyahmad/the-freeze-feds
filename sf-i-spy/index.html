SF I-Spy 🌁

Walk the streets of San Francisco. Every 8 seconds the world freezes, and you get 5 seconds to click what Claude spied.

When you press Start, your browser asks to share this tab, so Claude can see each frozen frame. Nothing is recorded or saved.
